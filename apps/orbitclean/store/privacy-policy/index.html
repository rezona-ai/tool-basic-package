Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Last updated: November 10, 2026
In short: Orbit Clean scans and cleans files on your device. Your files, file names and scan results never leave your phone, and we do not run our own servers that collect your data. The app is free and shows ads; our advertising partners collect limited device data to deliver those ads, as described in Section 4.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the Orbit Clean Android app (“Orbit Clean”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. It applies to the app distributed on Google Play under the package name com.kexing.portvanguard. By using the app you agree to this Policy.

2. What the app does on your device

Orbit Clean helps you find and remove files you no longer need: leftover installers, temporary and log files, thumbnail caches, empty folders, large files, duplicate files and recent files. It also shows storage and basic device information (such as CPU, memory and battery status).

  • All scanning happens locally. To find files, the app reads file names, sizes, dates and folder structure, and calculates content fingerprints (hashes) to detect duplicates. This processing happens only on your device. We do not upload your files, file names, file contents or scan results.
  • Nothing is deleted without your confirmation. Files are only removed after you select them and confirm the deletion.
  • Device information stays on the device. Storage, CPU, memory and battery details are displayed to you and are not sent to us.

4. Information collected by advertising partners

Orbit Clean is free and supported by ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, Google’s mobile advertising platform. Through AdMob, ads may also be provided by third-party ad networks and bidders that Google works with. When an ad is requested or shown, Google and these partners may automatically collect information such as:

  • Your device’s advertising ID (Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or city level)
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, time zone, network type, app version
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown, whether they were clicked, and related diagnostics

Partners use this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted, show ads tailored to your interests. This collection is governed by each partner’s own privacy policy, for example:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Ad technology providers that may serve ads through AdMob

We do not receive information from these partners that identifies you personally. We may receive aggregated reports (such as the number of ad impressions and revenue) that do not identify individual users.

Install attribution and measurement (Adjust)

We use Adjust, a mobile measurement service, to understand which advertising campaigns lead to app installs and to measure ad revenue. Adjust collects your device’s advertising ID, Google Play install referrer information, IP address, device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, language and app version), the dates of app installs and sessions, and the revenue earned from ads shown in the app. Adjust processes this data on our behalf and does not receive your files or scan results. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we pass your advertising consent choices to Adjust. See Adjust’s privacy policy.

Your advertising choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path may vary by device).
  • If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

5. Information we collect ourselves

We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos through the app. We do not run our own analytics or tracking servers; install attribution and ad measurement are handled by Adjust as described in Section 4.

If you contact us by email (for example via “Feedback” in the app), we will receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to respond to you and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

The app stores a few settings locally on your device (such as whether reminders are enabled and when you last cleaned). This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

You can change your choice at any time from the app’s Settings. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

7. Users in the United States

We do not sell personal information for money. You can opt out at any time by opting out of personalized ads or deleting your advertising ID as described in Section 4.

8. Children’s privacy

Orbit Clean is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention and security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we handle, such as feedback emails, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
