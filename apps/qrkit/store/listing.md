## 完整说明

```
QR Atelier makes QR codes for the things you share most, and reads the QR codes hiding in your screenshots and photos.

CREATE
• Links: turn any web address into a code people can scan with their camera
• Wi-Fi: let guests join your network without typing the password (WPA/WPA2/WPA3, WEP or open, hidden networks supported)
• Contact cards: share your name, phone, email and company in one scan
• Text: notes, codes or short messages
• Save the code as a sharp PNG image or share it straight to any app

READ FROM PHOTOS
• Pick a screenshot or photo with a QR code in it
• Open links, copy Wi-Fi passwords, add contacts to your address book, call numbers or write emails in one tap
• Works with dark and light codes

RECENT
• Your latest codes stay on the home screen so you can show or reuse them
• Delete one, or clear the list any time

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything happens on your phone. Your codes, photos and the text in them are never uploaded
• No account, and no storage, photo, camera or contacts permissions. You pick photos with the Android photo picker and choose where to save images

QR Atelier is free and supported by ads.
```
