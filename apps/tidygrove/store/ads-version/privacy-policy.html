Tidy Grove

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026 · Last updated: November 16, 2026
In short: Tidy Grove scans and tidies only the folders you choose, entirely on your phone. It never uploads your files, needs no storage permission, and keeps only a few settings on your device. It shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device information as described in Section 4.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tidy Grove Android app (“Tidy Grove”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. It applies to the app distributed on Google Play under the package name com.example.tidygrove. By using the app you agree to this Policy.

2. What the app does on your device

  • Folders you choose. When you pick a folder in the Android folder picker and tap “Allow”, the app can list, open, copy, move, rename and delete files in that folder only. You can see and remove allowed folders any time in Settings → Folder access.
  • Sweeps. To find leftover files, duplicates and large files, the app reads file names, sizes and dates in your allowed folders, and reads file contents only to compare files of the same size when looking for duplicates. This happens on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Deleting and organizing. Files are deleted, copied, moved or renamed only after you confirm, and only inside the folders you allowed.
  • Phone health. The app shows storage, memory, battery and system details read from Android. These are displayed on screen and not stored or shared.

3. Permissions we request

PermissionWhy we need it
Folders you choose (Android folder picker) To sweep and organize files in the folders you allow. This is not a storage permission: the app cannot see any other folder, and you can remove access in Settings → Folder access.
Notifications (Android 13 and later) To send optional check-in reminders when storage runs low or leftover files pile up. You can decline, or turn reminders off in Settings.
Internet and network state To load advertisements.
Advertising ID Used by our advertising and measurement partners to deliver and measure ads and to attribute app installs (see Section 4).

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, call logs, SMS or precise location.

4. Information collected by advertising partners

Tidy Grove is free and supported by ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, Google’s mobile advertising platform. Through AdMob, ads may also be provided by third-party ad networks and bidders that Google works with. When an ad is requested or shown, Google and these partners may automatically collect information such as:

  • Your device’s advertising ID (Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or city level)
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, time zone, network type, app version
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown, whether they were clicked, and related diagnostics

Partners use this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted, show ads tailored to your interests. This collection is governed by each partner’s own privacy policy, for example:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Ad technology providers that may serve ads through AdMob

We do not receive information from these partners that identifies you personally. We may receive aggregated reports (such as the number of ad impressions and revenue) that do not identify individual users.

Your advertising choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path may vary by device).
  • If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

5. Information we collect ourselves

We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos through the app. We do not run our own analytics or tracking servers.

If you contact us by email (for example via “Feedback” in the app), we will receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to respond to you and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

The app stores a few settings on your device only: whether reminders are on, when you last ran a sweep (so reminders are not sent too often), and the folders you allowed (kept by Android). This information never leaves your phone, and app backup is turned off.

6. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before our advertising partners use your data for personalized advertising, as required by the GDPR, the ePrivacy Directive and Google’s EU User Consent Policy. You can change your choice at any time from the app’s Settings. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data about you on our own systems, most requests about ad data should be directed to the relevant advertising partner; we are happy to help you with that.

7. Users in the United States

We do not sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act), allowing advertising partners to collect identifiers for targeted advertising may be considered “selling” or “sharing” personal information. You can opt out at any time by opting out of personalized ads or deleting your advertising ID as described in Section 4.

8. Children’s privacy

Tidy Grove is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention and security

Since the content you work with in the app never leaves your device, we do not retain it. Information collected by advertising partners is retained according to their policies. We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we handle, such as feedback emails, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time, for example when we add features or change advertising partners. We will post the updated version on this page and change the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app or on its Google Play page.
