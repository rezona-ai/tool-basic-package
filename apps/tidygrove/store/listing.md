## 完整说明

Tidy Grove keeps the folders on your phone neat. Share the folders you want it to look after, run a sweep, and clear out what you no longer need. Nothing is deleted until you confirm.

**Sweep in one go**
- Weeds: leftover APK installers, temporary and log files, cached preview thumbnails and empty folders.
- Doubles: files saved more than once. Each set keeps its first copy unless you choose otherwise.
- Big branches: files over 50 MB, largest first, so you can decide what stays.
- Pick whole groups with one tick, or tap "Look" to review file by file before you clear anything.

**Your folders, organized**
- Browse the folders you shared in a thumbnail grid.
- Press and hold to pick files, then copy, move, rename, share or delete them.
- Create folders and search by name.
- Find files by kind: photos, movies, audio, documents, compressed files, or anything added this week.

**Phone health at a glance**
- See used and free storage, memory, battery level and health, chip and Android version.

**Gentle check-ins (optional)**
- A reminder when storage runs low or leftovers pile up. Turn it off any time in Settings.

**Privacy first**
- Tidy Grove only sees the folders you choose with Android's folder picker. It asks for no storage permission and never uploads your files.
- Remove a folder's access any time in Settings → Folder access.

Tidy Grove is free and supported by ads.
